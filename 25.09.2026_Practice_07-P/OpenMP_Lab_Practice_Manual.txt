 Task 1.3:
 During intensive mathematical processing, a brief spike in CPU usage is observed (reaching up to 40% of total system capacity on the CPU LOAD graph).
 All available threads distribute computations across the processor cores in parallel;
 subsequently, the `.join()` synchronization barrier concludes the parallel region, and the load returns to its initial level.
